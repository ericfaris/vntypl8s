9 · Accessibility notes

  • Focus rings are never removed — input:focus shows outline: 2px solid var(--accent). Tab through the tile picker above to check.
  • Tiles carry aria-label ("Add A"); stickers and the paperclip are aria-hidden — decorative only.
  • --text on --panel and --accent on the primary button's dark ink both clear WCAG AA (≥4.5:1) — verified when the tokens above were picked.
  • Every sound call is wrapped so a blocked/missing clip never breaks play — the game is fully playable silent (see the mute button above).
  • Nothing secret ever renders on the TV — plate length shows as dots while writing, never characters.
